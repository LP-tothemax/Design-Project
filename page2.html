Hockey Gear Costs

Overview

Hockey equipment can be very expensive when buying new gear, but players can save money by buying used equipment. The price depends on the brand, quality, and level of protection.



Helmet

A new helmet can cost between $80 and $300 depending on the brand and safety features. Used helmets are cheaper, usually around $20 to $100, but may have wear and less protection.



Shoulder Pads

New shoulder pads usually cost $70 to $250. Used ones can be found for $20 to $80. Expensive pads offer better protection and comfort.



Gloves

Hockey gloves cost about $50 to $200 new. Used gloves are often $15 to $60. Higher-end gloves last longer and protect hands better from slashes and impacts.



Skates

Skates are one of the most expensive parts of hockey gear. New skates can cost $150 to $900. Used skates may cost $50 to $200, but they may not fit perfectly or have sharp blades.



Protective Pads (Shin, Elbow, Pants)

These usually cost $50 to $250 each when new. Used sets can be $20 to $100. More expensive gear provides better impact protection and durability.



Goalie Equipment

Goalie gear is the most expensive. New sets can cost $800 to over $3000. Used goalie gear may still cost $200 to $1000, but it is much cheaper than buying new.



Conclusion

Hockey can be an expensive sport, but buying used equipment helps reduce costs. However, newer gear usually offers better safety and lasts longer.
