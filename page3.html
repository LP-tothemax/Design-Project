Overview

Hockey is a fast and physical sport. Players can get injured from hits, falls, and collisions with the boards. Some injuries are more serious and can affect a player for a long time.




Concussions

Concussions happen when a player gets hit in the head or takes a hard impact. They can cause headaches, dizziness, and memory problems. This is one of the most serious hockey injuries.

Neck and Spine Injuries

Neck and spine injuries are very dangerous. They can happen from hard collisions or awkward falls and may affect movement and nerves.

Broken Bones

Broken bones can happen in the arms, legs, ribs, or collarbone. They are usually caused by hard hits or crashing into the boards.

Shoulder Injuries

Shoulder injuries happen often in hockey because of body checks and impacts with the boards. They can include sprains or dislocations.
